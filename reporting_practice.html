Verb Patterns: Reporting — Practice

Instructions: Report what each person said. Keep the same meaning. In the box, write the complete sentence — from the first word to the last, including any words that are given — and press Check.

The exercise comes in sets of three, one for each pattern: you get less help each time — first words, then only the first word, then nothing. The final set mixes all the patterns.

⚠ Remember: after a past reporting verb, move the tense one step back and change pronouns and time words. That is optional after say / tell.
